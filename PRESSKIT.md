## BIOGRAPHY

Juan Falibene's journey is one of deep passion, exploration, and an endless loop of listening, selecting, mixing, and sharing music. From an early age, he showed a profound affinity for sound, immersing himself in the rhythms of **Soul, Funk, Modern Soul, Rare Groove, and Jazz**. His focus has always been on creating meaningful connections through music, driven by a simple but powerful love for sharing it with others.

Alongside his foundation in organic sounds, Juan maintains a strong connection to club culture. His sets are a seamless fusion of organic and electronic elements, drawing heavily from **Disco and House music**. He excels at blending the raw essence of sampled material with the vibrant energy and texture of the dancefloor, keeping audiences captivated wherever he performs.

Known for his compelling sessions, Juan held a prominent residency at **Café Berlin Music Club in Madrid between 2016 and 2019**. During this period, he shared the stage with renowned international artists such as **Folamour, Hugo LX, Nickodemus, Pabels, and Manu Desrets**. His dynamic presence extends to regular radio sessions and guest mixes with international broadcasting stations across Madrid, Barcelona, Glasgow, and London.

Currently based in **Barcelona**, Juan continues to channel his creative energy into new opportunities, curating listening sessions, vinyl-only events, radio broadcasts, and creative music projects.
