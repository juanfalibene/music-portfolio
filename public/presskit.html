FULL BIOGRAPHY

Juan Falibene's journey is one of deep passion, exploration, and an endless loop of listening, selecting, mixing, and sharing music. Immersing himself in Soul, Funk, Modern Soul, Rare Groove, and Jazz, his focus is on creating meaningful connections through sound and sharing it with others.

Alongside organic sounds, Juan maintains a strong connection to club culture. His sets are a seamless fusion of organic and electronic elements, drawing heavily from Disco and House music. He excels at blending sampled material with the vibrant energy and texture of the dancefloor.

Held a prominent presence at Café Berlin Music Club in Madrid (2016–2019), sharing the stage with Folamour, Hugo LX, Nickodemus, Pabels, and Manu Desrets. Collaborating with radio stations across Madrid, Barcelona, Glasgow, and London, he is currently based in Barcelona curating unique listening sessions and creative collaborations.
